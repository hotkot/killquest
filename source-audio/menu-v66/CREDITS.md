# Iron at the Gate — menu theme v66

Original 112 BPM, two-minute low-D chug composition/arrangement, bass, drums and mix created for KillQuest. No Doom MIDI or melodies were used.

Sampled guitar: **Distorted guitar Palm mute off and on.ogg**, by **Skimel**, 2023-08-29. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Distorted_guitar_Palm_mute_off_and_on.ogg

Changes: recorded open/palm-muted A power chords cropped into individual articulations, pitch-transposed, gated, EQ-filtered, panned and sequenced into new riffs; new bass/drums added; mixed, normalized and encoded to MP3.
