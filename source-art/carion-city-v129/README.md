# Carion City empty walkthrough prototype

25,600 world pixels, exactly twice the 12,800px Forest. New Game → map → Carion City → character selection (one to four players). Available immediately for testing; other unfinished regions stay locked. The new game story video remains, but Forest's video and elder dialogue do not play in City. Three-second walk-in, empty rage, forward-only camera, rear-player tether. Reaching the citadel shows completion with replay/menu choices.

Six unique generated panoramas: outskirts, gatehouse, market, damaged residential quarter, prison yards and citadel approach. No mirroring or adjacent image reuse. Five soft overlap transitions share the same cobblestone plane and muted comic palette; the original half-speed backdrop was replaced in v130 with twelve fixed world-space scenery sections (see ../carion-city-v130/README.md). Scenery includes solid carts/stalls/wells/rocks, breakable sacks/baskets/crates/barrels and three breakable lane barricades. Food appears only after a food container breaks. No enemies, boss, encounter locks, pits, section-title overlays or newly connected campaign-map routes.

Built-in ImageGen prompt set: one 3:1 side-on panorama per district, grim 80s fantasy comic outlines and painted cel shading, muted grey/brown/purple/rust palette, consistent overcast light and horizontal ground plane, empty cobblestone walking band across lower third, no characters/text/UI, neutral stone edges for transitions, district architecture varying across width without tiling/mirroring. Original PNGs retained here; geometry-preserving WebP delivery copies in dist/assets/city.

Validation: full model traverse using movement and heavy attacks; zero spawns over the entire route; solid and destructible props; forward-only camera and backtrack clamp; four-player spacing/reset; map confirmation and character-selection launch; six loaded image paths, five transition canvases, city render/camera alignment and cleanup. Existing Forest entrance, guard, knight, projectile and loading checks pass. Browser preview was denied by its approval gate; no workaround used. No EXE build.

Current layout: v131 replaces city overlap blending with eleven tailored opaque architectural joins, adds three down-right passages and vertical camera follow, and binds movement to painted street corridors. See ../carion-route-v131/README.md.
