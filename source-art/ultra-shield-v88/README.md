# Garran: Ultra Shield Bash rage

Full rage now triggers a forward, grounded shield charge rather than the old leaping radial slam. Uses the same I / RB / R1 input and existing normalized shield-bash sprites. No character PNGs were changed or rescaled. A short shield windup leads into fast cycling charge poses and a standing recovery. Golden sprite afterimages and existing spark/debris sprites trail behind him; the old knight ground-slam overlay is removed. Kael's slam and Lyra's arrow rain are preserved, including simultaneous co-op effects.

The cast locks facing and lane, spends the rage bar once, and protects Garran through the cast. Windup .28s, travel .90s, recovery .34s. Maximum forward travel 1,280 world pixels, clipped to arena/level boundaries, permanent obstacles, pits and the existing Forest co-op screen leash. The damage sweep covers the entire traveled line, with 85px lane depth and 96px leading shield reach. The sweep catches targets between updates without tunneling, hits each target once for 200 damage, and throws ordinary enemies forward 300px with their normal 35-point landing impact. Bosses take damage while keeping their armor/attack state. Enemies behind the starting point or outside the lane are not hit.

Destructible props in the swept lane break and keep their normal container-only drops; permanent scenery remains solid. Arrows in the traversed lane are cleared. Hit-stop cannot stall the active charge. Per-cast hit/prop sets preserve once-only damage, score and ownership. Afterimage sprites are pooled during the cast and disposed on completion, reset or character changes.

Validation: `checks/ultra-shield.cjs` covers both facings, fine/coarse updates, full-distance travel, lane/range exclusions, one hit plus fall damage, common-enemy deaths, destructibles/potion drops, arrows, permanent barriers, pits, boss armor, cast immunity, co-op ownership, Forest leash and pose selection. Scene tests verify normalized ghosts, reuse/reset cleanup, no old knight blast, and simultaneous Kael/Garran specials. Existing knight, Lyra, rage, score, Forest campaign and player-normalization checks pass. Browser visual/controller playtesting was unavailable.

`charge-preview.gif` is an offline composition of the actual combat model's positions/pose timeline and existing game assets; it is a preview rather than a browser capture. The timeline is retained in `preview-timeline.json`. Hosted web update only; no Windows EXE build.
